# Upstream attribution

Ferrum translates the Silver and Silicon projects maintained by the Viper team
at ETH Zurich and their contributors. Source notices are retained in translated
modules and copied resources. The Mozilla Public License 2.0 text is provided in
`LICENSE.txt` and in each translated crate.

The precise reference revisions and repository URLs are in
`porting/sources.toml`; every translated module retains its pinned source link.
Copied resource provenance and SHA-256 checksums are in `porting/resources.toml`.

Native module assembly and orchestration follow `PLAN.md`. Port status and
remaining implementation gaps are recorded in `porting/modules.toml`; a module
declaration or passing compiler check is not a verification correctness claim.
